\begin{abstract}
Reinforcement learning and synthetic data generation have shown promise for improving LLM agents in general, but for training a particular model to perform well in a particular target environment, existing approaches either train on broad, general-purpose synthetic environments without targeting specific model deficits, or optimize directly on the target environment where reward is sparser and multiple capabilities interfere when learned jointly. We observe that failures across different task instances often stem from missing capabilities, specific abilities each of whose absence constitutes a distinct, recurring failure mode. Once isolated, each deficit can be trained for with far denser signal, converting the agent's failures into a direct lever for its improvement. We propose a framework that automates this process end-to-end. It identifies recurring capability deficits through contrastive trajectory analysis, synthesizes a programmatically verifiable training environment for each, trains a modular adapter per capability via reinforcement learning, and composes only the relevant adapters at inference time, creating a closed loop in which failures automatically generate the targeted training signal needed to correct them. On challenging agentic multi-turn tool-use benchmarks, our framework achieves up to X\% improvement over state-of-the-art synthetic data and environment generation methods with significantly less training. Further, we demonstrate that accuracy scales with both the number of capabilities and training compute.
\end{abstract}
